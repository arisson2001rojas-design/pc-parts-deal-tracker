# Open data attribution

The PC component names, specifications, part numbers, and retailer identifiers
imported by this project are derived from
[BuildCores OpenDB](https://github.com/buildcores/buildcores-open-db).

BuildCores OpenDB is licensed under the
[Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/).
PriceBuddy does not redistribute BuildCores price data. Current prices are
collected separately from retailer pages or approved APIs configured by the
operator.
